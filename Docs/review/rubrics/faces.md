# Rubric: faces

For `faces-*.png`, `expressions.png`, `face-styles.png` and `creator-face*.png`.

- **F1** [look] On every skin tone, are the brows, eyes and mouth clearly readable?
- **F2** [metric] Does every generated character clear the minimum brow-vs-skin luminance contrast? *(G1.10 sweep, not built)*
- **F3** [look] Do eye whites stay white and catch-lights stay bright, in light and in shade?
- **F4** [look] Does the face's shadow form one clean, deliberate shape (no diagonal cut through an eye or the nose), as in the reference frame? *(from G1.4)*
- **F5** [metric] Over a full light turntable, does the face shadow move one way and mirror correctly? *(from G1.4; V6 monotonicity, not built)*
- **F6** [look] At town distance, do small marks (blush hatching, freckles) avoid reading as dirt or stubble?
- **F7** [look] Does each expression read as intended (neutral, happy, delighted, shy, sad, angry, surprised)?
- **F8** [look] Is the face free of the warm and red shadow-edge bands the rest of the skin gets? *(from G1.2)*
- **F9** [look] Is every face free of features overlapping or poking through (brows through bangs, glasses through the face)?
